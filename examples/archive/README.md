# Archive

Historical sketches, fragments, and design notes from the development of Detour.
They are kept for context but are **not expected to parse or check** with the
current grammar — many predate it (e.g. the old `theorem foo:` headers or
`for propositions …` without `all`), and some are mid-file fragments.

Notable items:

- `N-total.dt` — the induction proof for addition totality, stored with its
  module prelude out of order. The repaired, checkable version lives at
  [`../sum-induction.dt`](../sum-induction.dt).
- `nmn.dt` — an early `prove`-automation experiment, superseded by
  [`../sum-auto.dt`](../sum-auto.dt).
- `lang-examples/` files — sketches of a different surface language.
